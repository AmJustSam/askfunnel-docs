All AskFunnel prices are in US dollars (USD). Unless the checkout says otherwise, your plan, renewals and add-ons are charged in USD.

## What this means for you

- **Your total:** shown before you pay. The **Order summary** on the checkout page shows the amount you will be charged, including any tax.
- **Your bank:** may convert the charge. If your card or bank account uses another currency, your bank or card provider converts the amount at its own exchange rate. Some banks add a foreign transaction fee. These come from your bank, not from AskFunnel, so ask your bank if you have questions.
- **Tax:** prices do not include tax. Tax is added at checkout where it applies, based on your country.
- **Currency setting:** there is none in AskFunnel. You cannot choose a different currency in your account.

Your invoices show the amount you were charged. See [Billing, renewals and invoices](/billing/billing-and-invoices).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My bank shows a different amount than the price">
    Your bank may have converted USD to your local currency or added a foreign transaction fee. The amount on your AskFunnel invoice is what AskFunnel charged, normally in USD. Your bank statement shows its own converted figure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total is higher than the plan price">
    Tax, add-ons or a prorated amount for an upgrade can be included. The **Order summary** at checkout lists each line before you pay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I would like to pay in another currency">
    Email support and tell us what you need. We will tell you what is possible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
