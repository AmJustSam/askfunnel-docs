Fourteen days go quickly. This checklist covers what most teams want to prove before they subscribe: a funnel that looks right, leads that arrive where you need them and follow-up that works.

<Tip>
  - **Pick the plan with the features you want to test.** For example, multiple emails in one email flow, Meta Conversions API, live calendar tracking and webhooks need Growth or Scale. See [Choose the right plan](/billing/choose-a-plan).
  - **You can switch for free.** Move your trial to another plan at any time without paying.
  - **Plan your AI credits.** Trials include 100 AI credits per month, so use them on what matters most.
</Tip>

## Your trial checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your first funnel">
    Start from a template or from scratch, then write questions that fit your audience. See [Create your first funnel](/funnels/create-your-first-funnel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it and send a test lead">
    Open your funnel link, answer the questions as a visitor would and submit. Then check the lead in **Leads**. See [Share your funnel](/funnels/share-your-funnel) and [View and manage leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send leads to the tools you use">
    Connect the places your team works. Google Sheets, HighLevel and tracking pixels are on every plan, and webhooks need Growth or above.

    - [Send leads to HighLevel](/integrations/highlevel)
    - [Send leads to Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets)
    - [Track ads with Meta Pixel](/integrations/meta-pixel)
    - [Send leads with webhooks](/integrations/webhooks)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your sender and follow-up emails">
    Verify your own sender email early, so you can test real emails before the trial ends. Then build an automated email for new leads. See [Add and verify a custom sender email](/messages/custom-sender-email) and [Send automated emails](/messages/automated-emails).

    Starter allows one message per email flow. To test a sequence with delays, use a Growth or Scale trial. See [Email sequences](/messages/email-sequences).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alert your team">
    Send an alert to your team when a new lead comes in, so nobody finds out late. See [Team alerts](/messages/team-alerts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your numbers">
    Once visitors arrive, open **Analytics** to see visitors, conversion rate and where people drop off. Use it to improve weak steps before your trial ends. See [Funnel analytics](/analytics/funnel-analytics).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on your plan">
    Compare your usage with the plan limits in [Check your plan and usage](/billing/plan-and-usage). When you are ready, follow [Upgrade your plan](/billing/upgrade) to subscribe. Subscribing charges you that day and ends the rest of your trial.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens if you need more time

You are not charged if you do nothing. After your 14 days, you have a 7 day grace period to subscribe. See [How the 14-day free trial works](/billing/free-trial) for the full timeline.
