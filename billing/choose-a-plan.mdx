Starter, Growth and Scale are self-serve, and each one starts with a [14-day free trial](/billing/free-trial) with no credit card. Enterprise is set up with our team.

Prices are in US dollars (USD) and do not include tax. Tax is worked out at checkout from your country and shown in the order summary before you pay.

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - Limits are shared by your whole organization. Leads, live funnels, team seats and AI credits are counted across all of your workspaces, not per workspace.
  - Yearly billing is paid up front. You pay for the whole year at once. The yearly price is 10 times the monthly price, which is where "2 months free" comes from.
</Warning>

## Which plan fits you?

| If you expect | Choose |
|---|---|
| Up to 100 leads a month, 1 or 2 live funnels and just you | Starter |
| Up to 300 leads, up to 10 live funnels and up to 3 people | Growth |
| Up to 1,000 leads, up to 20 live funnels and up to 5 people | Scale |
| 5,000 or more leads, more than 20 live funnels or a bigger team | Enterprise |

- **Starter** is for solo creators getting started with one or two funnels.
- **Growth** suits creators and teams who are scaling up. It adds the Meta Conversions API, live calendar tracking and webhooks, lets you send more than one email per email flow and removes the AskFunnel branding from your funnels.
- **Scale** is for high-volume funnels and agencies. It keeps everything in Growth and raises the limits to 20 live funnels, 1,000 leads a month and 5 team seats.
- **Enterprise** is for agencies and brands at scale, with custom limits, white-label on request, priority support and a Success Manager. Click **"Contact us"** on the Enterprise plan to book a call.

## Need more room on your plan?

You can add extra leads, AI credits, live funnels or team seats to Starter, Growth or Scale without changing plan. Choose them from the drop-down lists on the plan page. Each option shows its extra monthly price, and your total updates before you pay.
