Show only the leads from a certain day or period by adding a date filter to your **Leads** table. You filter on the **Created** field, which is the moment each lead submitted your funnel.

**Before you start**, you need:

- A funnel with leads, and a role that can see them: Global Admin, Workspace Admin or Contacts Manager. See [View and manage your leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads).

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - Dates follow your browser's time zone. A lead that arrived late in the evening for you is counted on that day. Analytics uses UTC (standard world time) instead, so day totals can differ. See [Read your funnel analytics](/analytics/funnel-analytics).
  - Your filter is saved for this funnel, in this browser. Teammates do not see it. Click **View options**, then **"Reset view"** to remove every filter, sort and group.
</Warning>

## Filter by a date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter">
    Open your funnel's **Leads** tab and click **Filter** in the toolbar.

    ![The Filter button in the leads toolbar, highlighted](/images/leads/filter-by-date/filter-button.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition for Created">
    1. Click **"Add filter"**. A row appears that starts with **Where**.
    2. In the first box, choose **Created**.
    3. In the second box, choose how to compare:
       - **is:** leads created on that day.
       - **is before** and **is after:** leads created before or after that day, not including the day itself.
       - **is on or before** and **is on or after:** the same, but including the day itself.
    4. Click the third box (**Pick a date**) and choose the day on the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    The table updates right away. The number on the **Filter** button shows how many conditions are active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common examples

- **Today's leads:** **Created**, **is**, today's date.
- **Leads since a campaign started:** **Created**, **is on or after**, the start date.
- **Leads between two dates:** add two conditions and leave **and** between them. First **Created**, **is on or after**, the start date. Then **Created**, **is on or before**, the end date.
- **Recent leads in one status:** add another condition with **Status** and **is any of**, then pick the status. See [Track leads with statuses](/leads/contact-status).

If your form has a date question, you can filter on that field too. It appears in the same list of fields.

<Tip>
  To put the newest or oldest leads first instead of filtering, click **Sort**, then **"Add sort"**, choose **Created** and pick **First → Last** or **Last → First**.
</Tip>

## Clear the filter

Open **Filter** and click the trash icon (**Remove condition**) on a row, or click **Clear all**. If no leads match, the table says **No leads match your filters** and offers a **"Clear filters"** button.
