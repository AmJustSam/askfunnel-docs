Add your own column to a funnel's **Leads** table to track extra information your funnel does not collect, such as a second phone number, a budget or an internal customer ID. You fill in a custom field by hand.

**Before you start**, you need:

- A role that can change fields: Global Admin, Workspace Admin or Contacts Manager. Content Editors do not have access to lead data.
- A funnel with its **Leads** tab open. See [View and manage your leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads).

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - Custom fields belong to one funnel. Create the same field again in each funnel that needs it. A funnel can have up to 300 fields.
  - Custom fields are for your own tracking. They are not available as rules or personalization in [automated emails](/messages/automated-emails).
</Warning>

## Create a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new field">
    1. Open your funnel's **Leads** tab.
    2. Click **Fields** in the toolbar.
    3. Click **"New field"** at the bottom right of the panel.

    ![The Fields button and the New field button, both highlighted](/images/leads/custom-contact-fields/new-field-button.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the field and choose its type">
    1. Type a **Field name**, for example "2nd phone number". A name is required.
    2. Open the type list and choose what the field will hold. See [Field types](#field-types) below.
    3. Optionally click **Add description** to explain what the field is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the field">
    Click **"Create field"**. The new column is added to your table. Scroll to the right to find it, or look for it in the **Fields** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the values">
    Click a cell in the new column, type the value and press **Enter** or click away to save. For select fields, pick from the list. You can add a new option from the list with **New option**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Field types

- **Single line text:** short answers and quick notes.
- **Long text:** longer notes. Turn on **Enable rich text** if you want bold, lists and links.
- **Number:** numeric values such as quantities or budgets.
- **Decimal:** exact amounts such as prices.
- **Single select:** a list where you pick one option. Click **Edit options**, add your options, optionally pick a color for each and click **Save**.
- **Multi select:** a list where you can pick several options.
- **Date**
- **Checkbox**
- **Email**
- **Phone**
- **URL**
- **Assignee:** pick one or more teammates.

## Edit or delete a field

Hover over the column header, click the small arrow and choose **Edit field** or **Delete field**. When you edit a field, click **Save changes** to keep your edits. Only fields you created can be edited or deleted. Fields that come from your funnel's questions and form fields change when you edit the funnel, and **Deal Value** can only be hidden.

<Warning>
  Deleting a field removes the column from your table. If you add a field with the same name later, the old values do not come back.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting
