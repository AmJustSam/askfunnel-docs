AskFunnel helps you collect and qualify leads with short, interactive funnels instead of long web forms. A funnel is a series of steps that asks people one question at a time. At the end they leave their contact details, and the lead appears in your dashboard with every answer attached.

## How a funnel works

- **The hook:** Someone opens your funnel and sees a headline written for their problem.
- **The questions:** They answer simple questions by choosing buttons or image cards. It feels like a short quiz, not a long form.
- **The qualification:** Questions about budget, goals or timeline show you who is serious. You can also turn on [lead scoring](/funnel-settings/lead-scoring) to rank leads by their answers.
- **The lead capture:** At the last step, they enter their contact details. You choose which fields to ask for.

## Why use a funnel instead of a form

- **One question at a time:** People are not asked for everything at once, so the funnel feels lighter than a long form.
- **Answers come with the lead:** Every lead keeps the answers that qualify it, so you know who to call first.
- **A faster start with AI:** Choose **"Generate with AI"** when you create a funnel, answer three quick questions, and AI builds a first version you can edit.

## What you can do with AskFunnel

- **Manage your leads:** See every lead with its answers, update its status, add notes and export your list. See [View and manage your leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads).
- **Follow the numbers:** See visitors, conversions and where people leave your funnel. See [Funnel analytics](/analytics/funnel-analytics).
- **Send leads where you work:** Connect tools such as [HighLevel](/integrations/highlevel), so new leads arrive there automatically.
- **Work with clients:** Keep each client's funnels and leads apart in their own [workspace](/workspaces/what-is-a-workspace).

## Who AskFunnel is for

- **Marketing agencies:** Give each client a workspace, build funnels for them, and let clients view their leads without changing your funnels.
- **Real estate professionals:** Ask about property type, location and budget before you pick up the phone.
- **Service businesses:** Solar installers, fitness coaches, financial advisors and other businesses that need to know what a customer wants before they quote.
