Most questions have a step-by-step answer in this Help Center. If you cannot find yours, email the AskFunnel support team.

## Search the Help Center first

Use the search box at the top of this page. The guides cover:

- Creating and managing funnels
- Funnel settings such as domains and social previews
- Leads, analytics and integrations
- Workspaces, team access and billing

## Email support

Write to [support@askfunnel.com](mailto:support@askfunnel.com). The more detail you include, the faster we can help. Please tell us:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead, including any error message
- Which funnel or workspace it affects
- The email address you use for AskFunnel
- A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

<Warning>
  Never send passwords, API keys, tracking tokens or other private credentials in an email.
</Warning>
