In a 30-minute call, a member of the AskFunnel team shows you the product and answers your questions about your own funnels.

## Book your call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking page">
    Go to [cal.com/askfunnel/growth-discovery](https://cal.com/askfunnel/growth-discovery).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    Choose a time that suits you and confirm your booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You receive a calendar invite with the meeting link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You will get more out of the session if you [create your account](/getting-started/create-account) first and think about what you want to build, for example the type of leads you want and the questions you would ask them.
</Tip>

## If you cannot make it

Use the reschedule or cancel link in your booking confirmation email, so the time can go to someone else.
