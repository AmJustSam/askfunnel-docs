AskFunnel replaces the form on your landing page with a short, interactive funnel. These are the most common ways to use it, so you can decide where to start.

## Qualify leads before you talk to them

Ask about budget, timeline or needs before people submit their contact details. You see the answers next to each lead, so your team can start with the people most likely to buy. Turn on [lead scoring](/funnel-settings/lead-scoring) to rank leads by their answers.

## Run lead funnels for many clients

If you run an agency, AskFunnel gives each client their own space.

<img src="/images/getting-started/use-cases/client-workspaces.png" alt="The Your workspaces page with four client workspaces, each showing its team size, funnels, leads and conversion rate" width="1808" height="1858" />

- **Keep each client separate:** Create one [workspace](/workspaces/what-is-a-workspace) per client, so their funnels and leads stay organized and private.
- **Build together:** [Invite your designers and copywriters](/workspaces/give-access) to work on funnels with you.
- **Give clients their leads:** Invite clients as [Contacts Managers](/workspaces/contacts-manager). They can view, update and export their leads, but they cannot change your funnels, and they do not use a team seat.

## Sell high value services

Funnels work well when a customer needs a few questions answered before you can help.

- **Real estate and property:** Ask about property type, location and budget to find serious buyers and investors.
- **Local services:** A solar installer can ask about the monthly electricity bill and roof space to see which homes are a good fit.
- **Consultations and quizzes:** Ask a few questions about what someone needs, then [let them book a call](/integrations/calendars).

## Get more from your paid ads

Send your ad traffic to a funnel and track what happens next.

- **Track your ads:** Add a pixel (a small piece of code that reports visits to an ad platform) to your funnel, such as [Meta Pixel](/integrations/meta-pixel), [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics) or [TikTok Pixel](/integrations/tiktok-pixel).
- **Find what to improve:** [Funnel analytics](/analytics/funnel-analytics) shows your conversion rate and the steps where people leave, so you can improve your questions and your ads.
