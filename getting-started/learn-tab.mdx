The **Learn** tab holds 18 short video lessons that are built into AskFunnel. Each one is about one to two minutes long and shows one task on screen, such as creating a workspace or testing a funnel. Every lesson has a matching help article in this Help Center, so you can watch first and read the details after.

With the lessons, you can:

- **Watch a task done step by step.** Each lesson follows a short list of steps.
- **Jump to the part you need.** Every lesson has a list of chapters with their times.
- **Read instead of watch.** Every lesson has a written walkthrough and captions.
- **Pick up where you stopped.** AskFunnel remembers your place in a lesson.

**Before you start**, you need:

- To be signed in. See [Sign in to AskFunnel with Google or an email link](/getting-started/sign-in).
- An active plan or free trial. The lessons are inside the app, so after a trial ends you see the page about your plan first.

Every role can open the lessons.

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - The lessons show a fictional business with demo data. Your screens show your own funnels and leads. If a screen in a lesson looks different from yours, follow the help article for the current steps.
  - A lesson counts as watched when you have played about nine tenths of it. Skipping ahead does not count. Your **Watched** marks and your place are saved in the browser you use, so they do not follow you to another browser or device, and your teammates do not see them.
  - The **Learn** button is hidden while you are inside the funnel builder. Go back to your funnels first.
</Warning>

## Watch a lesson

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Learn tab">
    Click **Learn** (the book icon) in the top bar. On a small screen, only the icon shows. The page is titled **Learn AskFunnel**.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: images/getting-started/learn-tab/learn-button.png | The top bar of the Funnels page of the Summit Realty Group workspace, with the Learn button (book icon and the word Learn) next to the notification bell and the plan badge. Highlight: the Learn button. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a lesson">
    - Click a topic: **All lessons**, **Getting started**, **Build & publish**, **Tracking & leads** or **Analytics**.
    - Or type in **Search lessons**. It looks in the title, the description and the steps of every lesson.

    Each card shows the topic, the length, the title, a short description and your status: **Watch lesson**, **Continue lesson** or **Watched**.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: images/getting-started/learn-tab/lesson-library.png | The Learn AskFunnel page with the topic buttons All lessons, Getting started, Build & publish, Tracking & leads and Analytics, the Search lessons box and a grid of lesson cards, one marked Watched and one marked Continue lesson. Highlight: the topic buttons and the Search lessons box. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it and try it">
    Click a card to open the lesson. Under the video you find:

    - **In this lesson:** the chapters. Click a time to jump to it.
    - **Try it in your workspace:** the steps of the lesson as a list, with a button **"Open my funnels"** that takes you to your funnels. On the first lesson, the button says **"Open workspaces"**.
    - **Read the walkthrough:** the whole lesson as text.
    - **Previous lesson** and **Next lesson:** move through the series. The list **Keep learning** at the right shows every lesson.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: images/getting-started/learn-tab/lesson-page.png | A lesson page for Test your funnel before sending traffic: the video player on the left with its In this lesson chapter list and the Try it in your workspace checklist below it, and the Keep learning list of lessons on the right. Highlight: the In this lesson list and the Try it in your workspace panel. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the matching help article">
    Use the table below to open the help article that goes with each lesson. It has the exact labels, the limits and the answers to common problems.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I know it is working?

- The video plays, and the chapter list jumps to the time you click.
- After you play most of a lesson, its card in the **Learn** tab shows **Watched**.
- If you stop part way, its card shows **Continue lesson** and the video starts again where you left off.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not see the Learn button">
    It is hidden inside the funnel builder. Go back to your funnels page and look again. On a small screen, look for the book icon without the word. You also need to be signed in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see This lesson couldn’t play">
    Check your internet connection and click **"Try again"**. The written walkthrough under the video works even when the video does not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My lessons show Watch lesson, but I watched them">
    Your progress is saved in the browser where you watched. A different browser, another device, a private window or cleared browser data starts empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I opened a lesson link from the Help Center and landed on Your workspaces">
    You were not signed in. Sign in, click **Learn** in the top bar and choose the lesson.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The lesson does not match what I see">
    The lessons use a fictional business, and the product may have changed since a lesson was recorded. Follow the matching help article in the table above for the current steps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a page about my trial instead of the lessons">
    The lessons are part of the app, so they need an active plan or free trial. See [Upgrade your plan](/billing/upgrade).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no lesson for my question">
    Search this Help Center, or email support. See [Contact AskFunnel support](/getting-started/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
