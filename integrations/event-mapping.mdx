Choose which standard Meta or TikTok event each funnel step sends, so the ad platform understands what the step means and can use it to optimize your ads.

Your tracking pixels (small pieces of code that report what people do on your funnel to an ad platform) get an event for every step a visitor takes in your funnel. Out of the box, each step is sent as a custom event named after the step, and the form submission is sent as the platform's own lead event. A standard event is one of the platform's fixed event names, such as **Lead** or **Contact**.

With event mapping, you can:

- **Choose the event for any step.** Pick a page view, an answer, a button click, or the start or end of your lead form.
- **Map each platform on its own.** The same step can send **Contact** to Meta and **SubmitForm** to TikTok.
- **Map only what matters.** Steps you do not map are still sent as custom events, so nothing is lost.

**Before you start**, you need:

- A saved Pixel ID in [Meta Pixel](/integrations/meta-pixel) for Facebook and Instagram ads, or in [TikTok Pixel](/integrations/tiktok-pixel) for TikTok ads. A mapping only works once that platform's Pixel ID is saved.
- Mapping is not available for [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics), which uses fixed event names.

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - If you use Meta revenue tracking, do not map a step to **Purchase**. Both would send a Purchase, and buyers would be counted twice.
  - You can have up to 200 mappings in total across Meta and TikTok.
  - When your cookie banner is on, events are only sent for visitors who accept marketing cookies. See [Cookies and consent](/funnel-settings/cookies-and-consent).
</Warning>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pixel's settings">
    1. From your main dashboard, find the funnel you want to change.
    2. Click the three small dots (**...**) on that funnel's row, then click **"Funnel Settings"**.
    3. On the left side menu, click **"Integrations"**.
    4. Under **Tracking**, click **"Settings"** on the **Meta Pixel** or **TikTok Pixel** card.
    5. Scroll down to **Map events to standard events**.

    <img src="/images/integrations/event-mapping/add-entry.png" alt="The Map events to standard events section of the Meta Pixel settings with no entries yet and the Add entry button highlighted" width="2000" height="513" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an entry">
    1. Click **"Add entry"**.
    2. In the first dropdown, **Funnel event**, pick the step. You can type to search.
    3. In the second dropdown, **Standard event**, pick what the platform should call it, for example **Contact** or **Lead**.
    4. Add more entries for more steps. To remove one, click the **X** at the end of its row.

    <img src="/images/integrations/event-mapping/two-entries.png" alt="Two mapping entries, Answer: This week to Lead and Lead form started to Contact, with the two dropdowns of the first entry highlighted" width="2000" height="659" loading="lazy" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **"Save changes"** in the bar at the top. If your funnel is published, AskFunnel shows **Settings saved. Your live funnel is updated.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I know it is working?

- The mapping rows are still there after you save and reopen the settings.
- Open your funnel in another tab, accept the cookies and click through to the mapped step. In Meta Events Manager or TikTok Events Manager, open your pixel's **Test events** tab. The step arrives under the standard event you chose, not as a custom event.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My mapped event does not show in Meta or TikTok">
    Check these, in order:

    - The pixel card shows **Connected**, and a **Pixel ID** is saved. Without one, AskFunnel warns that mappings do not take effect.
    - Both dropdowns of the entry are filled in. A half-filled entry is ignored.
    - You clicked **"Save changes"**.
    - You accepted marketing cookies on the funnel, if your cookie banner is on.
    - You reached the step in that visit. Each step is sent when the visitor gets there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The first dropdown shows (removed funnel action)">
    The page, answer or button that entry pointed to was deleted from your funnel. Click the **X** to remove the entry, or pick another step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I mapped the same step twice">
    If two entries use the same step, only the last one is used. Remove the one you do not want.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to map Google Analytics events">
    You cannot. Google Analytics only accepts event names made of letters, numbers and underscores, so AskFunnel sends fixed names such as **generate_lead** for you. See [Tracking your traffic with Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Buyers are counted twice in Meta">
    You have an entry mapped to **Purchase** and Meta revenue tracking is on, so both send a Purchase. AskFunnel warns you when this happens. Change the entry to another event, such as **Lead** or **InitiateCheckout**. See [Meta Pixel](/integrations/meta-pixel).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Manage your mappings

- **Change a mapping:** pick another event in the second dropdown and click **"Save changes"**.
- **Remove a mapping:** click the **X** at the end of its row and click **"Save changes"**. The step goes back to being sent as a custom event.
- **Pause tracking:** turn off the switch on the pixel's card. Your mappings are kept.

## The funnel steps you can map

The first dropdown lists the steps of your funnel. Each one is named like this:

- **Page view: Property type.** One entry for each page and result page. A page without a name is called **Untitled page 1**, **Untitled page 2** and so on, by its position.
- **Answer: Buy a home [What are you looking to do?].** One entry for each answer of a quiz question, followed by its page in square brackets.
- **Button: Learn more [Welcome].** One entry for each button, followed by its page. Buttons inside your lead form are not listed, because the form has its own two entries below.
- **Lead form started (first field typed).** The visitor typed in the first field of your lead form.
- **Lead form submitted (new lead).** The visitor submitted the form and a lead was created.

Mappings follow each step's ID, not its text. If you rename a page, answer or button, the mapping keeps working.

## What each step sends when you do not map it

| Funnel step | Sent to Meta and TikTok as a custom event named |
|---|---|
| Page view | Page Viewed: and the page name |
| Answer | Answered: and the answer text |
| Button | Button Clicked: and the button text |
| Lead form started | Form Started |
| Lead form submitted | Not custom. Meta gets **Lead**, TikTok gets **SubmitForm** |

Event names are cut to 50 characters. Each step is sent once per visit, so going back to a page or clicking the same answer again does not send it twice.

## The standard events you can pick

- **Meta:** Lead, CompleteRegistration, Contact, Schedule, SubmitApplication, StartTrial, Subscribe, ViewContent, Search, AddToCart, AddToWishlist, InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, Purchase, Donate, CustomizeProduct and FindLocation.
- **TikTok:** SubmitForm, CompleteRegistration, Contact, Subscribe, ViewContent, ClickButton, Search, AddToWishlist, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, CompletePayment, PlaceAnOrder and Download.

For a lead funnel, you might map:

- **Meta:** **Lead form started (first field typed)** to **Contact**, to see who shows intent before they submit. The form submission already sends **Lead** without any mapping.
- **TikTok:** **Lead form started (first field typed)** to **Contact**. The form submission already sends **SubmitForm** without any mapping.
- **Either:** an answer that marks a hot lead, such as **Answer: Buy within 3 months**, to **Lead** or **Schedule**.
