## Core concepts

Five words come up everywhere in AskFunnel.

- **Workspace:** a separate space for one business or client, with its own funnels, leads, team and settings. Agencies create one per client. See [What is a workspace?](/workspaces/what-is-a-workspace)
- **Funnel:** a short series of pages that asks visitors questions one at a time and ends with a form for their contact details. See [What is AskFunnel?](/getting-started/what-is-askfunnel)
- **Lead:** a person who filled in your funnel's form, saved with their contact details, every answer and where they came from. See [View and manage your leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads)
- **Lead score:** points you give to answers, shown as Cold, Warm or Hot, so the best leads stand out. See [Set up lead scoring](/funnel-settings/lead-scoring)
- **Integration:** a connection that sends each new lead to another tool, such as HighLevel, Google Sheets or Zapier. See [How integrations work](/integrations/how-integrations-work) and [Send your leads to HighLevel](/integrations/highlevel)
