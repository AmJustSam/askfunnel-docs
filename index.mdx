This help center shows you how to build a funnel, share it and follow up with every lead. New to AskFunnel? Follow the four steps below in order. They take you from a new account to leads arriving in the tools you already use.

## New to AskFunnel? Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up, set up your first workspace and start your 14-day free trial. No credit card needed. [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your first funnel">
    Build a short funnel from a template, add your questions and a lead form, and publish it. [Launch your first funnel](/getting-started/launch-your-first-funnel)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get and manage your first leads">
    Share your funnel link, then see every lead with their answers and score. [Share your funnel](/funnels/share-your-funnel) and [Manage your leads](/leads/view-and-manage-leads)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up automatically">
    Send each new lead to HighLevel, Google Sheets or any tool, and email them the moment they submit. [Connect your tools](/integrations/highlevel) and [Automated emails](/messages/automated-emails)
  </Step>
</Steps>
