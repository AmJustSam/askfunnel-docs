A workspace is where you build funnels and manage leads for one brand, business or client. Your account can hold as many workspaces as you need, so the work for different clients never gets mixed up. Your first workspace is created when you [create your account](/getting-started/create-account).

<img src="/images/workspaces/what-is-a-workspace/workspace-card.png" alt="A workspace card showing the workspace name, its member count, funnels, leads and conversion rate" width="1200" height="873" />

## What lives in a workspace

- **Funnels and leads:** Every funnel, and every lead it collects, belongs to one workspace.
- **Team and roles:** Each person you invite gets a role in a workspace. They only see the workspaces you add them to. Global Admins can open every workspace. See [Invite a teammate](/workspaces/give-access).
- **Workspace settings:** Sections for **General**, **Members**, **Calendars**, **Domains** and **Senders**. Open them from the **...** menu on a workspace card, then click **"Workspace settings"**. Global Admins and Workspace Admins can do this.
- **A funnel address:** Every workspace has a subdomain (the first part of its web address), so its funnels open at your-subdomain.askfunnel.com. You choose the subdomain when you [create the workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).

## Why use more than one workspace

- **Agencies:** Create one workspace per client, and give each client access to their own leads only.
- **Multiple brands:** Keep each brand or business entity in its own space.
- **Clear access:** Add people to just the workspaces they need.

## Important to note

<Warning>
  - Every plan includes unlimited workspaces. Live funnels, leads, team seats and AI credits are counted across all of your workspaces together.
  - Only Global Admins can create and delete workspaces.
</Warning>
